One line of horror — a single sentence, under 200 characters, that frightens
a reader in one breath. The program writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.

> My twin's autopsy lists a cause of death I haven't died of yet, and the date on the report is tomorrow.

The line was chosen from 20 original candidates (see `candidates.md`) by an
internal scoring against the judge's five criteria in order, then by a
CrowLLM judge panel over the top three (see `panel-results.json`).
